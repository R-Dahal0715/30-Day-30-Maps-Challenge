Day 3: Mapping Cities of Nepal Using Point Data

What I did
Added a city point dataset.
Identified longitude and latitude fields.
Imported point data into QGIS.
Used EPSG:4326 for geographic coordinates.
Styled city points.
Added city labels.
Combined cities with provinces and rivers.
Exported the map.

##Reflection
Day 3 introduced point data and helped me understand how locations can be represented using longitude and latitude. I learned that longitude is used for the X coordinate and latitude for the Y coordinate, (such a silly mistake but I make it every time..lol). I also learned that coordinate data must be assigned the correct CRS during import, otherwise it could create a hassle during the export.
